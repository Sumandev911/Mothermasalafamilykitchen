Our story

A family kitchen at the heart of Virar West

Mother Masala Family Kitchen began the way the best food always does — in a family kitchen, cooking to feed people properly. That same idea runs the restaurant today from Global City, Virar West: generous portions, honest spice, and recipes handed down and cooked fresh to order.

Our kitchen spans four flavour worlds under one roof — Malvani coastal seafood and gravies from the Konkan, hearty North Indian curries and dals, smoky tandoori kababs and platters, and wok-fired Indian-Chinese. Full vegetarian menu beside chicken, mutton, egg and seafood — more than 300 dishes in all.

For 6+ years we've served the families of Virar West — everyday dinners, weekend biryani, and the big functions. We deliver free across the local area on orders over ₹200, and we cater parties and bulk orders when it's time to feed a crowd.
