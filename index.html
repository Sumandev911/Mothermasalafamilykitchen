Our story

A mother's kitchen, for the whole of Virar

Mother Masala began the way the best food always does — in a family kitchen, cooking to feed people properly. That same idea runs the restaurant today: generous portions, honest spice, and recipes that travel from the Malvani coast through North India to the Indo-Chinese wok.

We're a family kitchen in Global City, Virar West, open every day till late. Whether it's a weeknight biryani for one or a tandoori platter for the whole table, it's cooked fresh to order and made to feel like home.
